# Reach

Who would use Cordon evidence on Arkiv: anyone who wants to check a Cordon verdict without trusting or reaching our server. That includes a developer whose install was blocked and wants to see why, a maintainer disputing a seal on their package, and anyone building another guard or dashboard on the same Sealed list.

What they need: a seal key or verdict from SealRegistry (public onchain), which carries the evidenceHash, and one arkiv_query call. No account and no key.

Honest status: Cordon is a hackathon prototype built for ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026. It has no users yet. The writer wallet is not funded yet, so no evidence entity exists on Arkiv until it is funded and a scan runs.
